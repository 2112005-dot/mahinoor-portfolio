01 / Profile

Counting the Hidden Environmental Cost of Buildings

Building Engineering & Construction Management sits between design and delivery, where a building has to be drawn, analysed, costed, and built. What holds my attention is the environmental cost of building—which is largely spent before anyone moves in and is rarely counted.

Life cycle assessment (LCA) is the tool I count it with, running through my undergraduate thesis and most of my published work. I favour research that stays close to the ground: built on real bills of quantities (BOQ) and project records rather than assumptions.

UNDERGRADUATE THESIS

Comparative LCA of High-Rise RCC Buildings in Rajshahi

Supervisor: Mehedi Hasan, Assistant Professor, Dept. of BECM, RUET

Assessing the cradle-to-grave environmental footprint of reinforced concrete towers in Rajshahi—evaluating material and water demand before occupancy. Models supplementary cementitious substitutions (Fly Ash, GGBFS) and Recycled Coarse Aggregate (RCA) across six impact categories (GWP, ADP, PED, AP, EP, RI) with full sensitivity analysis to establish a local low-carbon benchmark.
